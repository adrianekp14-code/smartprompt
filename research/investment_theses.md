## IESC — IES Holdings (Nasdaq) — status: DEEP RESEARCH / notification prepared, HELD
**Claim.** Electrical & technology contractor that has become a data-center infrastructure specialist (Communications: data-center
low-voltage; Infrastructure Solutions: custom power enclosures, switchgear/bus-duct; C&I). Controlled and capital-allocated by
Jeffrey Gendell (Tontine, 51.7%). EPS compounded from $1.55 (FY19) to $15.02 (FY25, pre-split).

**Evidence (SEC):** FY22→FY25 revenue $2.17B→$3.37B, op income $56M→$384M. Jun-26 qtr revenue $1,243M (+40% y/y), op income $179M.
RPO $2.8B contractual + $1.7B non-enforceable agreements. Acquired DBM Global (structural steel, ~$1.3B revenue) 2026-10-05 for ~$691M
($545M cash incl. $525M revolver borrowing + 430,974 shares). Post-split shares ~40.3M → market cap ≈ $6.4B at $158.45.

**Skeptic review (independent agent, 2026-11-08): survives with major caveats.**
- Jun-26 quarter flattered by "large, short duration jobs" (C&I margin 22.5% vs 11.2%) and a $26.2M securities gain (adj. EPS $6.70 vs GAAP $7.57).
- Cash flow helped by $180M rise in billings-in-excess; $311M securities portfolio inside a contractor.
- DBM adds leverage and lower-margin steel fabrication near a possible cycle peak.
- Tontine and CEO/CFO sold in May–Jun 2026 at $335–382 (post-split equivalent); buybacks negligible in FY26.
- Drawdown began after DBM closed (≈$339 on Oct 2 → $213 by Oct 23), before the Nov 4–5 midterm selloff.

**Scores:** Quality 3 · Growth 4 · Financial strength 3 · Competitive advantage 3 · Management 4 (capital allocation) / governance 2 · Valuation 4 · Downside risk 2 · Evidence 4.

**Scenarios (FY2028, per share; ~40.3M shares; pro-forma net debt ≈ $0.15B now):**
| Case | Revenue | Op margin | EV/EBIT | Net cash | Value/share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | $4.5B (data-center pause, DBM included) | 7% | 10x | −$0.15B | ≈ $75 |
| Base | $5.8B | 10% | 13x | +$0.5B | ≈ $199 |
| Bull | $6.5B | 12.5% | 15x | +$0.8B | ≈ $322 |

**Checkpoints:** FY26 10-K (expected ~Nov 20–Dec 9, 2026): Q4 C&I margin ≥ 13%; RPO ≥ $2.8B; operating cash flow ex-securities purchases positive;
DBM segment margin disclosed. Invalidation: C&I margins back to ~10%, RPO decline, Tontine sale, further debt-funded low-margin M&A.
