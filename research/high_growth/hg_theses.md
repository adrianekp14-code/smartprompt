## AEIS — Advanced Energy Industries (Nasdaq) — benchmark: SMH (semis) and SPY
- **Price:** $197.01.
- **Market cap:** $7.98B.
- **Net cash:** about $138M. Debt principal is $1.286B, including $1.15B of 0% converts due 2031 with a $508.78 conversion price.
- **EV:** about $7.84B.

**What it does.** Precision power conversion:
- RF plasma power for semiconductor equipment, 46% of Q3-26 revenue;
- AI and data-center server power, 37%;
- industrial and medical.

**1. What the market expects.**
- At a 15% target FCF margin, the price implies about 10% a year revenue growth for 10 years.
- At FCF margins of 8–10%, closer to history, it implies 15–18%. So the market is roughly fair *if FCF stays under 10%*.
- Q4 guidance: about $735M revenue, $3.90 non-GAAP EPS.
- The 49% drawdown tracks the AI-hardware and post-midterm sector selloffs. No company-specific trigger was found.

**2. What it could achieve in 5–10 years.**
- 2024 Investor Day targets for 2030: $3B revenue, 43% gross margin, $15 EPS. The Q4 run-rate is already about $2.9B and $15.6 non-GAAP.
- Capacity of more than $5B of revenue is planned by end-2028.

**3. Evidence expectations are too pessimistic.**
- Revenue +50% year on year.
- GAAP operating margin rose from 10.6% to 20.6%.
- About 13.7x EV / annualized Q3 GAAP EBIT.
- The company bought back stock at about $241 in October.

**4. Upside drivers.**
- Gross margin moving toward 43%+ as the Thailand plant ramps.
- An upcycle in leading-edge chip equipment.
- Release of $234M of year-to-date inventory build.

**5. What would prevent it.**
- Three customers are 54% of revenue and are not named.
- Server-power competition (Delta, Lite-On).
- Component shortages.
- Two cyclical end-markets peaking together.
- Diluted shares rose from 38.5M to 41.4M.

**6. Is the optimistic case priced?** On earnings, no. On cash flow, the price is about fair: TTM FCF is $70M, a 0.9% yield, and negative
after stock comp.

**Scenarios (2028; 41.5M diluted shares)**

**Next checkpoint:** Q4-26 results (Feb 2027). Watch FCF and inventory.

**2–4. Potential and upside.**

- Controlling holders (Priortech 20.7%, Chroma 16.8%).
- Insider sales in Aug-26.

**6. Is it priced?** About 33x TTM GAAP EBIT and about 35x P/FCF, so delivery of the guide is priced.
